What crosses the disciplinary boundary?

Energy levels and thermal transition rates determine information quality and the timing of errors. Those error histories, combined with an explicit damage law, determine survival.

A static information score omits temporal structure. Under instantaneous hazard, slow trajectories leave a fortunate surviving subset. Under multistage damage, frequent recovery can interrupt harmful exposure.

These are effective failure models, not a complete thermodynamic account of repair or autonomous life. The sensor starts in its unfailed stationary distribution; the damage clock starts fresh.
