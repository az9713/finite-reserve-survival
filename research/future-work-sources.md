# Sources and scope for the future-work agenda

This is a contextual source check for a research agenda, not an originality verdict for 24 future projects. The links identify established neighboring work. No future theorem, experiment, mechanism or biological capability is claimed to have been completed.

The project-specific gaps were checked against the current certificate, the independent-review scope, the validation notes and the [recent interpretation](../docs/continuum-assessment.md). In particular, the current checker does not independently recompute every PDE contraction; the exact constant optimizer, actual crossover endpoints and an explicit robustness region remain unproved. These are distinct from experimental calibration and biological extensions.

New searches included combinations of author names with stopping-time entropy production, first-passage fluctuation bounds, autocatalytic sets, chemical organization, self-replication, extinction, semantic information and compositional reaction/Markov networks. Secondary news, social-media interpretations and unrelated search results were not used as technical evidence.

This check did not systematically search every proposed extension or compare every theorem line by line. That is the first obligation when a particular future claim is selected. Source titles and abstracts establish neighboring prior work, not a certificate that our desired extension is new or technically attainable. The mathematical time-rescaling obstruction and fixed-Hamiltonian relative-entropy free-energy identity in the agenda are standard facts, not claimed contributions.
