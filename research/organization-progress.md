## Boundaries

The comparator is every constant paid-channel intensity in the common model, not every fixed architecture. Both arms have identical stipulated inventory and landscape, but an actual molecular recognition/fabrication cost has not been established. The spectator mechanism changes reverse rates as well as forward binding. The no-well ablation changes mechanics and mode energetics simultaneously. The open-bath diagnostic removes fluctuations and depletion together and is not initial-rate-matched. Uniform dispersion permits natural chance contacts.

No new general scientific concept, literature priority, molecular realization, replication, heredity or chemistry-to-biology barrier crossing is claimed. The existing Studies 01–05 manuscript remains a separate snapshot. Private project memory remains local.
