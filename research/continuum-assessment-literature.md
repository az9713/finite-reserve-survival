# Interpretation and next-theorem literature check

Checked 17 November 2026, local project date. This is a targeted contextual check, not a systematic review or a priority certificate. It accompanies the added interpretation in [the continuum assessment](../docs/continuum-assessment.md). No new physical experiment or parameter optimization was run for this documentation update.

## Question and limits

The existing certificate proves one continuum survival separation. The check asks which broader ideas are already established and whether a proposed mechanism theorem would require more than a standard control sensitivity calculation. It does not establish that a presently unspecified structural criterion is an open problem in the literature.

## Conclusion

The certificate strengthens mathematical evidence for the specified example. The broad ideas of fuel-governed organization, feedback, finite-time optimization, reversible spatial modeling and residual estimation are established. The exact combined benchmark may have a model-specific contribution, but absence of an exact match in this check cannot establish priority. A new structural theorem would need a sharper claim, a dedicated comparison of its hypotheses and conclusion, and external specialist scrutiny. The proposed sensitivity/Taylor lemma is standard supporting mathematics, not the novelty claim.
