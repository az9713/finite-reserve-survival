## Assessment of candidate novelty

This study derives a particular six-species closed network, its exact net resource identity, first-formation/persistence factorization, analytic one-complex allocation optimum, and a globally certified interior optimum over a two-parameter static family. The combination is a candidate benchmark contribution. The counting identity and the single-complex calculus are elementary consequences of the declared stoichiometry and rates. Their derivation here establishes correctness within the model, with priority unresolved.

The sharp resource bound has an explicit attaining reaction path. The numerical continuum optimum has a rational construction within the recorded certification tolerance. It does not identify an exact symbolic maximizer. The certificate covers the specified static kinetic family. It leaves different molecular mechanisms and adaptive barrier protocols for separate optimization.

No exact prior match was identified in the inspected sources. A model-specific numerical optimum, especially one found in an exploratory parameter family, does not by itself establish publication priority. Expert review should compare finite-inventory catalytic reliability, stochastic autocatalytic repair, absorption in reversible chemical networks, and rigorous optimization methods. The current paper is an assessable research draft with a reproducible candidate result.
