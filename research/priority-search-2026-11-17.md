## Claims deserving specialist priority review

- The exact fixed-word rate and switch at repair probability `2−sqrt(2)` for the specified two-unit checkpoint model.
- The exact two-round information-accuracy threshold `2/3` in that model.
- The autonomous example with a rigorously enclosed finite-time advantage over all constant sensing intensities and a different binary asymptotic optimum.

Bellman recursion, local detailed balance, Jensen's inequality, Perron–Frobenius theory, and uniformization are not novelty claims. The mathematical existence of an optimal member of a finite circuit class is elementary. The substantive computational statement is a certified ordering for a fully specified instance. A literature search cannot establish “never published”; human expert review is still required.
