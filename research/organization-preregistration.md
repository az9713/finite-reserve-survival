## Exact comparator scope

The fixed family is the complete interval of constant undirected paid-channel prefactors alpha in [0,1], common to every fuel edge. Alpha=0 includes passive organization and paid repair; alpha=1 includes full paid binding even at the last fuel molecule. All other material/landscape parameters are common and fixed ex ante.

The autonomous candidate uses s(b)=(b-1)/(N-1) on the edge whose free endpoint has b fuel molecules. It corresponds to spectator-fuel catalysis: free+2F <-> tethered+F+W. The reverse uses the same undirected edge multiplier. It senses through the stipulated chemical rate law, without a measurement record or external lookup/controller. Molecular realization and additional material costs beyond these idealized species remain unverified.

This is a comparison against best constant paid-binding intensity, NOT every time-homogeneous chemical network, all affinities, or all possible fixed organizations. The autonomous augmented generator is itself time homogeneous. Do not describe the restricted comparison as a universal victory over fixed physical chemistry.
