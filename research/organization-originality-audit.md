# Originality audit for paid spatial organization and repair

17 November 2026

**Decision.** Proceed as a model-specific comparison. Batch-fueled organization, fuel-dependent binding, self-healing during the assembly lifetime, and performance improvements over constant interactions each have direct precedents. The benchmark asks whether fuel-sensitive tethering improves first-failure survival when binding and repair draw on one closed reserve, compared with the best uniform tethering activity under the same resource and rate ceilings. The fixed family includes both paid tethering and the option of devoting the entire inventory to repair. The sources inspected leave that particular comparison open. A positive numerical gap would establish an advantage within the declared design family. Priority and generality remain unresolved. ## What the comparison needs to isolate

Start both arms uniformly dispersed in a periodic cube, in free conformation \(q=0\), with one healthy and one damaged complex and \(b=N=4\). The relative position evolves by thermal diffusion. A reversible switch to \(q=1\) changes the mechanical potential, with the same tether potential in all arms. Thermal and fuel-coupled channels can switch the conformation at fixed position. Paid repair and paid tethering share the same \(F/W\) inventory. The fixed-index family has a single paid-organization conductance \(\alpha\in[0,1]\) across all fuel edges, with the same passive binding and damage/repair channels throughout. The endogenous design changes that conductance from edge to edge through spectator fuel. At the free endpoint of an edge the factor is \((b-1)/(N-1)\). These limits and the specific physical geometry define the comparison. [Preregistered parameters](organization-preregistration.md)

The common event is uninterrupted survival through \(T=2\) with failure at the first visit to \(DD\). The benchmark tests \(S_A(T)-\sup_{\alpha\in[0,1]}S_\alpha(T)\). The functional complexes exist at launch, so tethering is a possible aid to maintenance. A separate claim about making the functional complexes from building blocks would require formation to be part of the outcome, as in the project's earlier well-mixed investigation. [Prior well-mixed assembly study](../docs/self-assembly.md) · [Prior Brownian maintenance study](../docs/spatial-repair.md)

The well-mixed project already uses a closed budget for both assembly and cooperative repair, an unconditional formation-and-maintenance objective, and a certified optimum over static parameters with a binding–repair coupling. The earlier spatial study already includes Brownian position, reversible paid repair, and first-collapse survival, with preassembled functional complexes and no organization channel. The extension here compares uniform and fuel-sensitive paid allocation to an explicit mechanical state. ## Attempts to reduce the proposed result to existing mechanisms

**The ledger follows directly from stoichiometry.** For net unit-fuel organization and repair currents \(J_O,J_R\), \(b(t)-b(0)=-J_O(t)-J_R(t)\) path by path. Bounds on net spending do not bound gross turnover when the reverse channels regenerate fuel. This identity is the same bookkeeping used in the well-mixed study and in the closed-reactor balances above. Matches in \(N\) must include matching the initial \(b,w\) and the fuel-to-waste free-energy scale. An independent hidden chemical store, a preloaded bound state, or an extra kinetic capacity would change the comparison.

**Detailed balance and spatial forces are standard.** Let \(s\) include health and binding labels, and let \(x\) have potential \(U_s(x)\) in thermal units. For the overdamped fixed-mobility generator \(D(\Delta-\nabla U_s\cdot\nabla)\), the mechanical invariant weight is \(e^{-U_s(x)}\) conditional on \(s\). For a local unit-fuel transition \((s,b)\leftrightarrows(s',b-1)\) with identity placement, a reversible weight \(\pi(x,s,b)\propto e^{-U_s(x)-E_s-\varepsilon b}/[b!(N-b)!]\) requires the forward/reverse ratio \(b e^{\varepsilon+E_s-E_{s'}+U_s(x)-U_{s'}(x)}/(N-b+1)\) for explicitly labelled states. A symmetric kinetic gate cancels in that ratio. A binding-dependent mechanical force requires the corresponding potential in this ratio or an equivalent balance construction. The finite-fuel launch can relax transiently under a globally reversible generator. Spatially heterogeneous nonequilibrium behavior during that relaxation follows from the initial condition. [Zhang and Isaacson, 2022](https://arxiv.org/html/2201.04099v3)

**A closed autonomous model is itself a fixed microscopic design.** Enlarging the state to retain fuel, binding, and location makes the generator time homogeneous. Its fuel-dependent conductance has an ordinary mass-action realization at the level of the stated species. For \(\mathrm{free}+2F\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{tethered}+F+W\), the reagent factors are \(b(b-1)\) on the way in, with the factor \(1/2\) for identical reactants absorbed into the rate constant, and \((b-1)(w+1)\) on the same edge in reverse. The extra factor \((b-1)/(N-1)\) therefore gates both directions. It cancels in their ratio whenever positive, and where it vanishes the paired paid edge is absent. The benchmark measures what that admitted coupling buys over a common scalar conductance; a larger design space containing that coupling has optimum at least as large as the tested fuel-sensitive design. The comparator can realize the same per-edge rate ceiling and the same spatial force. When the reservoir is clamped at a fixed composition, the spectator factor itself is a constant and can be reproduced by an admissible constant-conductance setting. A diagnostic that clamps only one reaction or uses unequal reservoir conventions would change the problem.

**More contact has opposing effects.** In the previous Brownian model let \(g\) be the contact gate. At an \(HD\) launch, \(S''(0)=\eta(3\eta/2+\kappa b\mathbb E g)\), so increased contact favors early survival if fuel is present. At an \(HH\) launch, \(S''(0)=-\eta(2\eta+\kappa w\mathbb E g/2)\), so increased contact can hasten functional loss when waste enables reverse repair. Organization also exchanges fuel with the repair reaction. These signs defeat a universal inference from increased binding occupancy to increased survival. At the null where both mechanical states share the same potential and repair gate, binding labels influence health only through changes in the fuel history. If neither organization nor repair depends on fuel exchange, their differences cannot affect a health-only payoff. [Prior Brownian calculation](spatial-repair-originality-audit.md)

**Short horizons already favor saving the first fuel.** On the finite spatial grid, the first two powers of the killed generator applied to the live payoff \(\mathbf 1\) are independent of the tether policy. The first is \(-\eta\) in \(HD\) and zero in \(HH\), and the second has the values in the preceding paragraph at a given \(x\). Starting uniformly in \(HD,b=N,q=0\), the difference in the third derivative between constant prefactor \(\alpha\) and \(\alpha=0\) comes from applying the paid tether edge to the common second generator image. If \(k_O\) is the paid attempt-rate coefficient, the difference is

\[
S_\alpha'''(0)-S_0'''(0)=-\alpha\eta\kappa k_O N\left\langle g^2e^{-\max(U_1,0)}\right\rangle.
\]

The fuel-sensitive design has the same third derivative as \(\alpha=1\), because both use the same conductance prefactor at full fuel. Paying for a tether removes a fuel molecule from potential repair before the tether's force can improve the encounter. The strict short-time penalty, where the gate has positive weight, leaves the ordering at the declared horizon unresolved. [Implemented generator](../src/purposeful/organization.py)

**First passage already has a standard computation.** For the common launch law \(\mu\), the survival is \(S(T)=\mu e^{TQ_{\rm live}}\mathbf 1\) on a lattice and has the analogous killed-semigroup expression in continuous space. Optimizing it numerically is a benchmark. A certified positive difference requires a lower enclosure on the fuel-sensitive design and an upper enclosure on the best constant conductance. A search over finitely many values supplies attained values and can locate candidate optima. Robustness to spatial refinement and numerical tolerances remains a separate question. [Verification design](organization-verification-design.md)

## Evidence needed to attribute a nonzero gap

- Give the common start, failure rule, baseline bounds, time horizon, and continuous design class. Include the thermal-binding-plus-paid-repair case \(\alpha=0\). Any no-tether experiment removes the common passive binding channel, so it is a mechanism ablation outside the specified constant-conductance family.
- Distinguish the gain over a fixed family from the gain due to geometrical localization. A matched spatial ablation that preserves fuel stoichiometry while removing the binding-dependent force or repair gate can test that mechanism. A finite-stock ablation can test resource feedback. Its inventory assumptions must be reported.
- Report the design and cost tradeoff if the paid path loses, ties, or wins only against weaker fixed materials. For a positive result compare the claimed margin against optimization, discretization, and sampling or propagation uncertainty. An expanded passive family may erase the margin.

The targeted search found direct precedents for each component and close comparisons on related outcomes. It found no primary source evaluating this exact combination of the closed reversible inventory, resolved motion, competing fuel uses, unconditional first-failure metric, and optimized uniform paid-channel conductance.
